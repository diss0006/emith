About Emith

"I prefer to define myself as a polymath, because I'm curious enough to explore many fields, and passionate enough to master whatever I pursue. "

I am Emith Dissanayake, an Interactive Media Design student at Algonquin College in Ottawa. I grew up in Colombo and came here to study the part of design that sits between the visual and the technical.

That means a project can go from user research and wireframes through to brand, layout and the front-end code that ships it, without the intent getting lost somewhere in a handoff.
