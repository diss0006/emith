I am an Interactive Media Design student with both the creative and the technical half of the job: work that looks like something, and work that actually holds up when someone uses it. That runs from user research and prototyping through to responsive front-end, with graphic design and branding alongside it.

I grew up in Colombo and moved to Ottawa to study the part of design that sits between the visual and the technical.
